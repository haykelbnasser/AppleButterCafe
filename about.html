Who We Are

Our Story

Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

Crafting Culinary Warmth

Apple Butter began as a simple idea: that a meal shared in a warm, welcoming space can change the tone of an entire day. From our first location in London to our newest home in the heart of Riyadh's Olaya District, we've carried that belief with us every step of the way.

"Good food has the power to make you feel at home anywhere in the world."

Our Riyadh kitchen is run by a passionate team dedicated to sourcing the finest seasonal ingredients — whether it's a perfectly brewed specialty coffee to start your morning, a nourishing grain bowl for lunch, or a relaxed dinner with people you love.

We believe great hospitality isn't just about the food on the plate — it's about the atmosphere, the detail, and the genuine care we put into every interaction.
